# Antwort an App Review (Guideline 2.1 – Information Needed)

Diesen englischen Text in App Store Connect als Antwort senden **und** in „App-Prüfung → Notizen“ einfügen.
Die Bildschirmaufnahme als Anhang an die Antwort hängen.

Hello App Review team,

thank you for the review. Please find the requested information below. The screen recording (captured on an iPhone running the latest iOS) is attached.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
The attached recording starts with launching the app and shows: account registration, creating a couple and the invite code, the home screen (day counter, mood, "Ich denk an dich" heart, question of the day), love notes, memories with a photo, wishes, dates and date ideas, settings, logout, login, and account deletion (Settings → "Konto löschen").

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
"Wir zwei" is a private app for couples (German-speaking adults in a relationship). Each partner uses the app on their own iPhone; both see the same shared space. It helps couples stay connected in everyday life: it counts the days they have been together, lets them send short love notes and a "thinking of you" heart, answer a daily question together, share their mood, and keep shared memories, wishes and important dates in one place.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
- Demo account: see the username and password fields of the App Review Information. This account already belongs to a couple with sample content.
- No email confirmation is needed. To try it yourself: tap "Neu hier", enter any email and password, then "Paar anlegen" (create a couple) to get a 6-letter invite code. A second account can join with "Ich habe einen Code".
- Account deletion: gear icon on the home screen → "Konto löschen". This immediately deletes the account and all shared data of the couple.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): user authentication (email and password), database, photo storage and realtime sync between the two partners.
No other services are used at runtime: no payment providers, no AI services, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app has no in-app purchases.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app is in German and offered in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. It works the same in every region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT
All content (notes, answers, memories, photos) is visible only to the two people of one couple, who connected deliberately with a private invite code. There is no public content, no feed, no search and no way to contact strangers. Each user can delete their own entries at any time, and deleting the account immediately ends the connection and removes all shared data. Users can contact us at prepitoys@gmail.com (also linked on our support page) to report any problem.
